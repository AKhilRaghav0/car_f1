# Sound Files for Enhanced F1 Car Mod

This directory contains all the audio files needed for the enhanced F1 car mod.

## 🎵 **Required Sound Files**

### **Engine Sounds**
- `car_f1_engine_idle.ogg` - Engine idle sound (looping)
- `car_f1_engine_running.ogg` - Engine running sound (looping)
- `car_f1_start.ogg` - Engine start sound
- `car_f1_stop.ogg` - Engine stop sound

### **Car Interaction Sounds**
- `car_f1_enter.ogg` - Sound when entering the car
- `car_f1_exit.ogg` - Sound when exiting the car
- `car_f1_crash.ogg` - Sound when car crashes
- `car_f1_horn.ogg` - Car horn sound
- `car_f1_tire_screech.ogg` - Tire screeching sound

### **Maintenance Sounds**
- `car_f1_refuel.ogg` - Sound when refueling
- `car_f1_repair.ogg` - Sound when repairing

## 📁 **Current Sound Files**
- `running.ogg` - ✅ Available (can be renamed to `car_f1_engine_running.ogg`)
- `start.ogg` - ✅ Available (can be renamed to `car_f1_start.ogg`)

## 🔄 **File Renaming Instructions**

To use your existing sound files with the new system:

1. **Rename** `running.ogg` to `car_f1_engine_running.ogg`
2. **Rename** `start.ogg` to `car_f1_start.ogg`
3. **Create** or **download** the missing sound files

## 🎧 **Sound File Specifications**

- **Format**: OGG (recommended for Minetest)
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
- **Channels**: Mono or Stereo
- **File Size**: Keep under 100KB for performance

## 🎯 **Audio Features**

- **3D Positioning**: Sounds change based on distance and direction
- **Dynamic Volume**: Engine sounds change based on speed
- **Looping**: Engine sounds loop seamlessly
- **Fade Effects**: Smooth sound transitions

## 📝 **Notes**

- The mod will work without all sound files, but audio features will be limited
- You can use placeholder sounds or create your own audio
- All sounds are automatically registered by the audio system
- Sound files are loaded when the mod starts
